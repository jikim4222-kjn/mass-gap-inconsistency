# Axioms

This file makes explicit a set of assumptions that are implicit in the standard
formulation of the Yang–Mills mass gap problem as posed by the Clay Mathematics Institute.
No additional assumptions are introduced.

The purpose of stating these axioms explicitly is to clarify which structural
conditions are required for the usual reading of the problem.

---

## A3 — Single Vacuum

**Statement.**  
The Yang–Mills theory admits a unique Poincaré–invariant vacuum state.
All physical excitations are defined as states above this vacuum.

**Remarks.**

- The existence of a unique vacuum is standard in the formulation of the problem,
  even when not stated explicitly.
- The notions of excitation, spectrum, and mass gap presuppose such a vacuum
  as a reference state.
- No multiplicity of vacua or superselection sectors is assumed.

---

## A4 — Intrinsic Four–Dimensional Mass Gap

**Statement.**  
The mass gap is an intrinsic property of the four–dimensional Yang–Mills theory.
That is, the gap is generated entirely within a single four–dimensional vacuum,
without reference to additional layers, sectors, or external structures.

**Remarks.**

- The Clay problem asks for a mass gap of the four–dimensional Yang–Mills theory itself,
  not one induced by embedding into a larger framework.
- Interpretations invoking additional layers or external structures are therefore
  excluded by the standard reading of the problem.
- This axiom does not assert how the mass gap is generated, only that it is
  internal to the four–dimensional theory.

---

## Notes on Scope

- These axioms are not proposed as physical postulates.
- They are intended solely as an explicit formulation of conditions implicit in
  the standard problem statement.
- Subsequent logical analysis examines the compatibility of these axioms with
  minimal and widely accepted physical observations.
