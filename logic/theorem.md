# Theorem

Under the assumptions A3 (Single Vacuum) and A4 (Intrinsic Four–Dimensional Mass Gap),
together with the physical facts F1 (Vacuum Energy Observation) and
F2 (Interlayer Interpretation of Gaps), a logical contradiction arises.

---

## Formal Statement

\[
A3 \;\land\; A4 \;\land\; F1 \;\land\; F2 \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \bot
\]

---

## Notes

- The theorem asserts a logical inconsistency, not a physical impossibility.
- No claim is made regarding the non-existence of a mass gap.
- The result concerns the compatibility of assumptions used in the standard
  formulation of the Yang–Mills mass gap problem with minimal physical observations.
