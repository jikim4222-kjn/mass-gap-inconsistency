# Remarks on Observations and Assumptions

This note addresses common interpretive responses to tensions between
theoretical assumptions and physical observations.
It is not part of the logical argument.

---

## Observations vs. Assumptions

When a theoretical formulation appears to conflict with observation,
two broad responses are possible:

1. Question the reliability or interpretation of the observations.
2. Re-examine the assumptions built into the formulation.

In the context of vacuum energy, the relative character of measurements
is a robust and widely accepted feature.
It is therefore natural to treat this as fixed background knowledge.

---

## Minimal Revision Principle

The inconsistency note adopts a minimal revision principle:
when a tension arises, assumptions should be relaxed before
discarding established observational facts.

This does not determine which assumptions should ultimately be
modified.
It only motivates making those assumptions explicit and subject
to scrutiny.
