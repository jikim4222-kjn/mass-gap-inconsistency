# Vacuum Energy and Relativity of Measurement

This note provides physical background on vacuum energy.
It is not used in the logical argument of the inconsistency note.

---

## Observational Character of Vacuum Energy

Vacuum energy is not accessed as an absolute quantity.
In practice, only differences between configurations or states
are physically observable.

Absolute contributions to vacuum energy depend on renormalization
schemes and subtraction procedures.
As a result, statements about vacuum energy are inherently relative
to a chosen reference.

---

## Structural Implication

This observational fact does not by itself invalidate the notion of
a vacuum state.
However, it places pressure on interpretations in which a single
vacuum is assumed to be a complete and self-contained reference
for all physically meaningful quantities.

The inconsistency note uses only this minimal and widely accepted
character of vacuum energy observations.
No assumptions are made about microscopic mechanisms or cosmological
models.
